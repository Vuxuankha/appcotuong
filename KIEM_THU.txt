CO TUONG AI - KIEM THU VA TINH TRANG (11/10/2026)

DA XAC NHAN:
- HTML/JavaScript: node --check thanh cong.
- server.js: node --check thanh cong.
- Node server thuc te khoi dong va cap phat cong localhost ngau nhien.
- Ma co nut Luu van va MO VAN DA LUU, goi loadGame va validateLoad.
- Khi nap Pikafish, app kiem tra su hien dien cua 4 tep: worker.js, pikafish.js, pikafish.wasm, pikafish.nnue.
- Che do du phong AI JavaScript van co trong ung dung.

CHUA XAC NHAN:
- Chromium end-to-end: lan chay tu dong bi timeout (khong tinh la PASS).
- Pikafish UCI/WASM thuc te: 4 tep chua tai duoc vi may tao goi khong ket noi duoc github.com.
- Doi thu AI, cham diem Pikafish, luat chieu lien tuc/lap the va cac thao tac day du: chua test E2E.

HUONG DAN:
1. Giai nen zip, cai Node.js LTS.
2. Chay CAI_ENGINE.bat voi ket noi GitHub de tai Pikafish (PowerShell can cho phep script).
3. Chay CHAY_APP.bat, app tu chon cong localhost.
4. Trong giao dien bam Nap Pikafish, sau do Kiem tra AI. Neu fail, app se bao tep thieu.
5. Neu chua cai engine, dung AI JS (AI tham khao).
