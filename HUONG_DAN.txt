CO TUONG AI - BAN NANG CAP TONG HOP

1. Giai nen vao thu muc rieng.
2. Cai Node.js LTS neu may chua co.
3. Nhap dup CHAY_APP.bat de khoi dong dia chi localhost cong ngau nhien, tranh mo nham ung dung.
4. Choi bang AI JavaScript khi chua cai engine Pikafish.
5. Neu co bo Pikafish WASM tuong thich, chay CAI_ENGINE.bat va nhan Nap Pikafish. Khong bao dam bo tai engine tai ve thanh cong vi phu thuoc nguon GitHub.
6. Cac nut moi: Phat lai (Nuoc truoc/Nuoc sau), Luu van, Xuat JSON, Nhap JSON, Luyen nuoc sai.
7. Nuoc di phai duoc hoan tat khi bot dang nghi; neu dang xem lai hay nhan Tro lai van.
8. Phan tich/diem JS la uoc luong, KHONG thay cho engine Pikafish. Luat chieu lien tuc va lap the phuc tap chua duoc giam dinh day du.
9. Chi nhap tep JSON do ung dung nay tao va co nguon tin cay.
